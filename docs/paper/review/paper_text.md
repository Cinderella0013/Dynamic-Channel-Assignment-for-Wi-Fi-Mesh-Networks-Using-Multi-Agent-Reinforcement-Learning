Abstract 
We propose EGAT-MAPPO for joint channel assignment and routing in multi-hop Wi-Fi mesh networks. 
Every node is an agent that chooses its downlink channel and its parent. The model combines an edge-
aware GATv2 encoder, two decision timescales and a residual prior from Ko's local-cost rule; it is warm-
started by behaviour cloning, trained with MAPPO and executed in a decentralised way with switch 
hysteresis. In a 20-node, six-channel simulator over 11 scenarios, we compare it with M0, Ko, Q-neutral, 
Yuan (GAT+DQN) and Li (GAT-A2C) on a pre-registered five-metric composite, with intervals that count the 
training-seed variance of both methods. EGAT-MAPPO improves on every baseline in its published form by 
at least 10%, significantly (Ko +19.5%, Li +16.0%, Yuan +25.6%), and meets all four pre-registered criteria. 
On fresh topologies it still improves on Ko by 15.1%. Most of the gain over Ko comes from fewer switches; 
when the baselines receive the same hysteresis, the margin falls to 4-8%. 
Keywords  Channel assignment  Routing  Graph attention  MAPPO  Wi-Fi mesh
